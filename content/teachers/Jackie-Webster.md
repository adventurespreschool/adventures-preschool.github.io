### About Jackie

Jackie Webster is an Assistant Teacher at Adventures Preschool.  She joined the Adventures Preschool Staff in 2026 and has been working with preschool-aged children since 2024.  Jackie is currently attending Des Moines Area Community College where she is working towards her Associate of Science degree.  She plans to continue her education at Iowa State University to earn her Bachelor's Degree.  Jackie enjoys helping young children learn, grow, and build confidence in a fun, caring and positive environment.  In her free time, she enjoys playing pickleball, taking her two Goldendoodles on walks, reading and spending time with her family and friends.
